Daily flow

  1. Doctor sets start time, total patients, online quota; stars priority patients.
  2. Patient books online → gets unique ID (TC-YYMMDD-001).
  3. Reception sees today's list; gives tokens in order of arrival.
  4. Doctor calls tokens in order — priority patients jump to the front.
  5. Patient tracks token, patients ahead & wait time.
